# Scene 3: Overview

## Purpose

Scene 3 continues the momentum of the bow-and-arrow sequence from Scene 2 and transforms the arrow’s release into the introduction of three interactive medallions.

The scene begins with action. The arrow that was drawn in Scene 2 shoots directly downward and passes behind all three medallions in one continuous movement.

Once the arrow completes its flight and settles behind the medallions, the pace shifts from movement to exploration.

The three medallions become the primary focus of the scene, with each functioning as an interactive button that opens its own modal.

---

## Narrative Goal

Scene 3 has two main story beats:

1. **The arrow completes its flight through the medallions.**
2. **The visitor is invited to explore the three medallions.**

The arrow should connect Scene 2 and Scene 3 as one uninterrupted action.

The visitor should not feel as though the arrow stops, reaches a new webpage section, and then continues traveling.

Instead:

**The bow releases → the arrow shoots → it passes behind all three medallions → it settles.**

Once that movement is complete, the scene becomes calm and interactive.

---

## Starting State

Scene 3 begins directly from the final action of Scene 2.

The bow has been fully drawn.

The arrow is pulled upward.

The bowstring is under tension.

Continued scrolling triggers the release.

At the moment of release:

- the bowstring snaps back into its resting position
- the arrow shoots downward
- the arrow enters the Scene 3 composition
- all three medallions are already positioned along its path

There should be no separate introduction or slow approach to the medallions.

---

## Opening Animation

The arrow completes its movement during the first portion of Scene 3.

It:

- shoots downward from above
- passes behind Medallion 1
- continues behind Medallion 2
- continues behind Medallion 3
- reaches its final resting position behind the full medallion stack

This should happen as one continuous shot.

The arrow does not stop at each medallion.

Once the arrow reaches its final position, the major movement ends and the scene settles.

---

## Medallions

Three medallions are stacked vertically along the arrow’s path.

Each medallion is an independent interactive button.

The arrow sits visually behind all three medallions, creating a continuous vertical line through the composition.

The arrow should remain visible:

- above the first medallion
- between the first and second medallions
- between the second and third medallions
- below the third medallion

The medallion artwork itself remains in front of the arrow.

---

## Ambient Animation

Once the arrow has settled, the scene remains alive through subtle continuous movement.

Ambient animation includes:

- outer gold glow around each medallion
- gentle cloud movement
- optional subtle atmospheric movement

The arrow remains stationary during the interactive portion of the scene.

---

## Medallion Glow

Each medallion should have a soft animated gold glow around its outer edge.

The glow should:

- maintain a faint resting halo
- gradually increase in brightness
- softly expand outward
- reach a gentle peak
- return to its resting state
- repeat after a short pause

The three medallions can use slightly offset timing so they do not pulse in perfect synchronization.

The glow should make the medallions feel active and clickable without becoming distracting.

---

## Medallion Interaction

Each medallion opens its own modal.

When a medallion is tapped:

- its outer gold glow briefly becomes brighter
- its corresponding modal opens
- the background scene remains in place
- the arrow remains stationary behind the medallions
- the visitor’s scroll position is preserved

When the modal is closed:

- the visitor returns to the exact same position
- the arrow does not replay its entrance
- the medallions remain in place
- ambient glow animation continues
- the visitor can open another medallion or continue scrolling

Opening the medallions is optional.

The visitor does not have to open all three modals to continue through the story.

---

## Scene Hold

After the arrow completes its flight, there should be a deliberate hold where no major scroll-driven movement occurs.

This is the primary interactive moment of Scene 3.

During the hold:

- the arrow remains behind the medallions
- all three medallions remain accessible
- their outer gold glows continue animating
- clouds continue subtle ambient movement
- the visitor can explore any of the three modals

The scene should remain stable for as long as the visitor chooses to stay.

---

## Scroll Behavior

The first portion of the scene completes the arrow’s flight.

The middle portion holds the completed medallion composition and allows interaction.

Continued scrolling beyond this hold begins the transition into the next scene.

The complete movement and exit choreography is documented in the **Scene 3 Transition** page.

---

## Ending State

By the end of the main Scene 3 composition:

- the arrow has completed its initial downward flight
- the arrow is stationary behind all three medallions
- all three medallions are fully visible
- all three medallions are interactive
- each medallion has an animated outer gold glow
- clouds continue their subtle ambient movement
- the visitor has been given time to explore the modal content

Continued scrolling begins the transition into the next story beat.

## Scene Handoff

Scene 3 settles with the arrow positioned behind the complete vertical stack of three medallions.

The medallions remain illuminated by their animated outer gold glows and are available for interaction.

The arrow remains still until the visitor continues scrolling.

That continued scroll begins the transition out of the medallion scene and into the next story beat.
