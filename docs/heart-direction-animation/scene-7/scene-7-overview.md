# Scene 7: Overview

## Purpose

Scene 7 is the final resting scene of the Heart Direction experience.

After the proclamation closes and clears the frame, the finale is fully revealed.

The scene brings together the final visual elements of the journey:

- the glowing sun
- four CTA buttons
- the rainbow
- Lain Doe Love on horseback
- the lower field and flowers
- surrounding clouds

Unlike the previous scenes, Scene 7 does not lead into another major story transition.

Its purpose is to give the experience a clear ending while offering the visitor several direct paths to continue engaging with Heart Direction.

---

## Narrative Goal

Scene 7 has two main goals:

1. **Complete the visual journey**
2. **Give the visitor clear next steps**

The final scene should feel like arrival rather than another reveal.

The major storytelling movement is finished.

What remains is a living illustrated tableau with subtle ambient motion and a set of clear interactive links.

The progression should feel like:

**Proclamation closes**

↓

**Final world is revealed**

↓

**Scene settles**

↓

**Environment continues living**

↓

**Visitor chooses where to go next**

---

## Starting State

Scene 7 begins immediately after the final transition from the proclamation.

By the time the scene fully settles, the following elements are visible:

- sun positioned at the top
- four CTA buttons stacked vertically
- rainbow arch
- Lain Doe Love on horseback
- lower landscape
- flowers and grass
- surrounding clouds
- blue sky background

All major entrance motion has finished.

The scene now shifts into its final ambient state.

---

## Sun Animation

The sun remains positioned near the top of the composition.

It should have subtle continuous animation that makes it feel radiant without distracting from the CTA buttons below.

Ambient behavior can include:

- gentle glow breathing
- slight changes in brightness
- very small movement or wiggle in the rays

The sun face itself should remain stable.

The rays provide most of the motion.

The effect should be restrained and continuous.

---

## CTA Buttons

There are four primary buttons:

1. **Be My Sweetheart**
2. **Read the Journal**
3. **Contact Me**
4. **Follow the Journey**

Once the finale has settled, the buttons remain physically still.

They do not need continuous floating or movement.

Each button functions as a direct link to its corresponding destination.

The entire illustrated sign should act as the clickable or tappable area.

---

## CTA Interaction

The buttons should clearly communicate interactivity without relying on constant animation.

Possible feedback includes:

- slight brightness increase
- soft highlight
- subtle glow
- tiny scale increase on hover or touch

Selecting a button should navigate directly to its destination.

The buttons do not open modals.

---

## Rainbow

The rainbow acts as a stable environmental element connecting the CTA area to the character scene below.

It should remain primarily still.

No major animation is required.

If additional life is desired, it can have extremely subtle light or texture variation, but it should not compete with the horse animation or CTA buttons.

---

## Lain Doe Love

Lain Doe Love remains mostly still in the final composition.

The character should feel grounded and composed.

The primary animation in the lower scene should come from the horse and environment rather than from large character movement.

Optional subtle polish may later include:

- slight fabric movement
- tiny hair movement
- subtle body breathing

These are not required for the initial build.

---

## Horse Animation

The horse provides the primary living motion at the bottom of the finale.

Its legs should move in a gentle repeating cycle.

The goal is not to make the horse gallop across the scene.

Instead, it should feel like:

- stepping in place
- lightly walking
- or gently prancing

The movement should create the illusion that the horse is alive while remaining anchored within the final composition.

---

## Horse Motion

The horse leg animation should:

- loop smoothly
- remain subtle
- avoid large body jumps
- preserve the rider’s stable position
- feel coordinated rather than jittery

The horse body may have a very small supporting movement to help sell the leg motion.

The final implementation can use separated leg assets, a short frame sequence, or another lightweight animation method.

The movement should prioritize smoothness and mobile performance.

---

## Grass and Field Animation

The lower environment should support the horse animation.

Grass and flowers should appear to move gently beneath and around the horse.

The effect should feel like environmental motion rather than the ground sliding underneath the character.

Possible behavior includes:

- gentle grass sway
- subtle flower movement
- light directional motion
- soft wind-like movement

The field should remain secondary to the horse.

---

## Cloud Animation

Clouds continue the ambient animation language used throughout the website.

They may:

- drift slowly
- move slightly vertically
- use independent timing between groups

Cloud movement should remain subtle and should never interfere with button readability.

---

## Ambient Animation

Once the final composition has settled, the primary ambient motion includes:

- sun glow
- slight sun-ray wiggle
- horse leg movement
- grass and flower movement
- subtle cloud drift

Mostly static elements include:

- CTA buttons
- rainbow
- Lain Doe Love
- major background structure

The scene should feel alive without feeling busy.

---

## Scroll Behavior

Scene 7 does not require another major scroll-driven narrative sequence.

Once the visitor reaches the final composition:

- the scene settles
- ambient animation continues
- buttons remain interactive
- the visitor may stay on the page or choose one of the links

The finale functions as the natural endpoint of the scrollytelling journey.

---

## Ending State

There is no additional story scene after Scene 7.

The final state is the complete illustrated tableau:

- sun gently glowing
- CTA buttons available
- rainbow stable
- Lain Doe Love positioned on horseback
- horse moving subtly
- grass and flowers moving
- clouds drifting

The visitor has reached the end of the Heart Direction experience and can choose where to continue next.
