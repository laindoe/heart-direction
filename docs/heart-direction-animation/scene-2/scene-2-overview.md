# Scene 2: Overview

## Purpose

Scene 2 introduces Lain Doe Love as the central figure inside the Heart Direction world.

The visitor arrives here immediately after traveling through the heart tunnel from Scene 1.

The scene should feel like a reveal and a meeting, not simply the next section of a webpage.

The visitor first encounters Lain Doe Love surrounded by clouds, with the glowing heart necklace acting as the primary interactive focal point.

The birds and “Meet Lain Doe Love” banner then float down from above and settle into the composition.

After the introduction has had time to register, continued scrolling reveals the bow and arrow from behind the character and clouds.

---

## Narrative Goal

This scene has two main story beats:

1. **Meet Lain Doe Love**
2. **Reveal the bow and arrow**

The first beat introduces the character and gives the visitor time to take in the composition.

The second beat introduces the bow and arrow as the next symbolic object in the story.

The bow and arrow should not appear immediately. They should remain hidden until the visitor has had time to actually meet the character.

---

## Starting State

Scene 2 begins as the visitor reaches the end of the heart tunnel.

The first visible elements are:

- Lain Doe Love
- surrounding clouds
- the heart necklace
- background environment

The following elements begin outside or behind the visible composition:

- left bird
- right bird
- “Meet Lain Doe Love” banner
- bow
- arrow

The character and clouds should feel as though they were already waiting at the end of the tunnel.

They do not dramatically animate onto the screen.

The visitor arrives at them.

---

## Opening Animation

As the visitor enters Scene 2:

- Lain Doe Love becomes fully visible
- the clouds settle into the composition
- the heart necklace begins blinking and glowing
- the birds and banner float down from above
- the birds and banner settle into their final positions

Once they are in place, the scene briefly holds.

This creates the full “Meet Lain Doe Love” composition before the next reveal begins.

---

## Ambient Animation

Once the opening composition settles, the scene remains alive through subtle continuous movement.

Ambient animation includes:

- clouds drifting gently
- birds floating independently
- banner subtly floating or swaying
- heart necklace blinking and glowing
- optional atmospheric movement in the background

The character should remain mostly still so the surrounding animation supports rather than overwhelms the introduction.

---

## Heart Interaction

The heart necklace is an interactive button.

It should visually communicate that it can be tapped through a repeating light animation.

The heart should:

- maintain a soft resting glow
- periodically brighten
- blink or flash gently
- return to its resting glow
- repeat after a short pause

When tapped, the heart opens a modal.

Closing the modal should return the visitor to the same point in the scene without resetting the scroll position.

---

## Scroll Behavior

After the banner and birds have settled, there should be a short hold before the next major animation begins.

As the visitor continues scrolling:

- the bow and arrow emerge from behind the character and clouds
- they move downward into view
- more of the bow and arrow are revealed as they clear the foreground layers
- they settle into their final position beneath the character

The bow and arrow should feel like they were physically hidden inside the composition rather than appearing through a fade.

The complete choreography is documented in the **Scene 2 Transition** page.

---

## Ending State

By the end of Scene 2, the full composition is visible:

- Meet Lain Doe Love banner
- left bird
- right bird
- Lain Doe Love
- surrounding clouds
- blinking heart necklace
- bow
- arrow

The major reveal animation has stopped.

Ambient animation continues.

The scene holds briefly before transitioning into the next story beat.

## Scene Handoff

Scene 2 ends with the complete character composition visible.

The bow and arrow are fully revealed beneath Lain Doe Love.

The heart necklace continues blinking.

The birds, banner, and clouds continue their ambient movement.

The viewer is now ready for the next story beat involving the bow and arrow.
