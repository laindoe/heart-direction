# Scene 6: Transition

# Transition Goal

The transition out of Scene 6 should happen **while the proclamation is closing**.

As the paper scroll rolls itself back up, the finale does not wait offscreen until the proclamation is completely gone. Instead, the final scene begins building underneath it in layers.

The movement should feel like one chapter being sealed while the next chapter rises into place.

The reveal order is:

**Proclamation begins closing**

↓

**Sun rises into view**

↓

**Buttons rise one by one**

↓

**Rainbow rises**

↓

**Lain Doe Love on horseback rises into the final composition**

↓

**Proclamation fully clears**

↓

**Final scene settles**

The transition should feel ceremonial and cumulative. Each new element should arrive after the previous one has had enough time to register.

---

# STARTING STATE

The transition begins after the visitor has reached the bottom of the fully opened proclamation and completed the final reading hold.

Visible:

- lower portion of the proclamation
- final Ten Grand declaration
- bottom scroll roller
- parchment filling most of the viewport
- exterior sky and clouds around the sides

The final scene is positioned below the proclamation but is not yet visible.

Continued scrolling begins rolling the proclamation closed.

---

# PHASE 1: PROCLAMATION BEGINS CLOSING

As the visitor continues scrolling:

- lower roller begins moving upward
- parchment begins rolling back into itself
- bottom proclamation content disappears first
- visible parchment becomes progressively shorter

The viewer is still primarily focused on the scroll.

The final scene should not immediately appear at full strength.

There should be a short closing lead-in before the first finale element emerges.

---

# PHASE 2: SUN BEGINS RISING

As the parchment continues shortening, the sun begins moving upward from below the viewport.

The sun is the first element from the finale to appear.

It should enter gradually.

At first:

- only upper rays may become visible
- then more of the rays appear
- then the sun face begins entering the frame

The sun should remain centered horizontally.

Its movement should feel like a literal sunrise beneath the closing proclamation.

---

# SUN ENTRY BEHAVIOR

The sun moves upward in response to scroll progress.

It should:

- remain horizontally centered
- maintain its illustrated proportions
- rise smoothly
- slow slightly as it approaches its final position

Avoid:

- spinning
- bouncing
- dramatic scaling
- fading into existence

The sun physically rises into the composition.

---

# PHASE 3: SUN SETTLES

The sun reaches its final position near the top of the finale composition.

It should settle while the proclamation is still continuing to close above or move away.

There should be enough time for the sun to register as the new visual anchor.

Once settled, the sun can begin subtle ambient animation.

Possible idle behavior:

- very slow glow breathing
- subtle ray brightness variation
- tiny warm shimmer

The sun itself should remain structurally still.

---

# PHASE 4: FIRST BUTTON RISES

After the sun has entered and mostly settled, the first CTA button rises from below.

The first button is:

**BE MY SWEETHEART**

The button should physically push upward into position.

It should not fade in.

As it enters:

- button moves vertically upward
- it approaches its final centered position
- movement slows before settling

The button should remain fully readable throughout the final portion of its movement.

---

# PHASE 5: SECOND BUTTON RISES

Once the first button has settled enough to register, continued scrolling brings in the second button:

**READ THE JOURNAL**

It rises from below and settles beneath the first button.

The first button remains in place.

The finale is now building vertically.

---

# PHASE 6: THIRD BUTTON RISES

Continued scrolling reveals the third button:

**CONTACT ME**

It rises into position beneath Read the Journal.

Again:

- existing buttons remain in place
- new button enters from below
- movement is vertical
- final spacing remains consistent

---

# PHASE 7: FOURTH BUTTON RISES

The fourth button is:

**FOLLOW THE JOURNEY**

It rises last.

Once settled, the full CTA stack is visible:

1. Be My Sweetheart
2. Read the Journal
3. Contact Me
4. Follow the Journey

Each button should arrive independently rather than the entire stack moving up as one prebuilt block.

This gives the finale a satisfying stepped reveal.

---

# BUTTON REVEAL RHYTHM

The buttons should feel like they are being presented one by one.

The rhythm should be:

**Rise**

↓

**Settle**

↓

**Next rises**

↓

**Settle**

Each button needs a small amount of separation from the next reveal so the visitor can understand what has appeared.

Avoid having all four buttons chase each other upward too quickly.

---

# BUTTON MOVEMENT

Each button should:

- begin below the visible area
- travel vertically upward
- remain centered
- slow as it reaches position
- settle without bounce

Avoid:

- lateral movement
- rotation
- elastic easing
- excessive scale changes

The illustrated signage already carries enough visual personality.

The movement can stay simple.

---

# BUTTON INTERACTIVITY

Once a button has reached its final position, it becomes active.

Do not make it clickable while it is still partially entering the frame.

Each button should have its own hover/touch state once settled.

Possible interaction cue:

- subtle glow
- small highlight
- tiny scale increase
- stronger border illumination

The exact button destinations can be implemented separately.

---

# PHASE 8: RAINBOW BEGINS RISING

Once the four buttons are mostly established, continued scrolling begins revealing the rainbow from below.

The rainbow should rise as a large environmental element.

At first:

- top of rainbow arch enters
- more bands become visible
- arch grows into the lower portion of the composition

The rainbow should feel like part of the landscape, not another button or overlay.

---

# RAINBOW MOVEMENT

The rainbow should:

- move straight upward
- remain centered
- preserve its illustrated proportions
- gradually reveal the world underneath the CTA stack

Its arrival begins shifting the finale from a UI-like CTA composition into a full illustrated ending scene.

---

# PHASE 9: CHARACTER + HORSE BEGIN RISING

As the rainbow moves farther upward, Lain Doe Love on horseback begins appearing beneath it.

The character and horse should be treated as one connected foreground assembly during the entrance.

They rise together.

The viewer should first see:

- upper portions of the horse and character
- then more of the bodies
- then the surrounding landscape

The character should not fade into existence.

She physically rises into the final composition.

---

# CHARACTER + HORSE ENTRY

The combined character/horse assembly should:

- move upward as one unit
- remain horizontally centered according to the final illustration
- preserve the relationship between rider and horse
- slow as it reaches final placement

Avoid separate entrance timing for the character and horse.

They are one physical scene element.

---

# PHASE 10: LANDSCAPE REVEAL

As the character and horse rise, the lower environment becomes visible.

This may include:

- flowers
- grass
- field
- lower clouds
- landscape details
- decorative foreground elements

The landscape should appear naturally with the rising final composition.

It does not need individual entrance animation for every flower or object.

The major reveal is the upward arrival of the environment.

---

# RELATIONSHIP TO THE CLOSING SCROLL

While the finale is building, the proclamation continues rolling closed and moving out.

The two actions overlap.

The progression should feel like:

**Old chapter compresses upward**

while

**New chapter expands upward from below**

This creates a visual exchange between scenes.

The proclamation should never obscure the finale once an element has reached its intended position.

---

# PHASE 11: PROCLAMATION FULLY CLOSES

During the finale reveal, the parchment eventually reaches its compact closed state.

At this point:

- proclamation text is completely gone
- upper and lower rollers are close together
- scroll is fully sealed

The scroll may briefly remain visible above the emerging finale.

Then it continues upward and exits the viewport.

---

# CLOSED SCROLL EXIT

Once fully closed:

- scroll moves upward
- it leaves the final scene behind
- no proclamation content remains
- the finale becomes completely unobstructed

Avoid fading the scroll out.

It should physically leave the frame.

---

# PHASE 12: FINAL SCENE TAKES OVER

Once the scroll has cleared, the full ending composition is visible.

Visible:

- sun
- four CTA buttons
- rainbow
- Lain Doe Love on horseback
- clouds
- landscape
- exterior sky

The finale is now fully established.

Major entrance movement stops.

Ambient animation can continue.

---

# FINAL SCENE AMBIENT BEHAVIOR

Once settled:

## Sun

- subtle glow breathing
- optional slight ray shimmer

## Clouds

- slow drift
- gentle independent movement

## Buttons

- mostly stable
- hover/touch interaction effects only

## Rainbow

- static
- optional extremely subtle texture/light movement

## Character + Horse

- primarily static

Optional later polish could include:

- tiny horse breathing
- subtle clothing movement
- small environmental motion

These are not required for the first build.

---

# VISUAL ATTENTION HIERARCHY

During the transition, attention should shift in stages.

## Stage 1

Closing proclamation

## Stage 2

Sun

## Stage 3

CTA buttons

## Stage 4

Rainbow

## Stage 5

Character + horse

## Final

Complete finale composition

This staged hierarchy prevents the ending from appearing all at once and becoming visually overwhelming.

---

# SCROLL BEHAVIOR

The entire transition should remain driven by scroll progress.

The visitor controls:

- proclamation closing
- sun rising
- button entrances
- rainbow entrance
- character/horse entrance
- scroll exit

Ambient animation remains time-based.

The viewer should be able to pause between reveals without the composition looking broken.

---

# REVERSE SCROLL BEHAVIOR

The transition should reverse cleanly.

If the visitor scrolls upward:

- character and horse move downward
- rainbow lowers
- buttons leave in reverse order
- sun moves downward
- closed scroll returns
- proclamation begins reopening

The scene should reconnect seamlessly with the proclamation.

No element should duplicate.

---

# IMPORTANT BUTTON REVERSE ORDER

When reversing the CTA reveal, the buttons should leave in reverse order:

1. Follow the Journey
2. Contact Me
3. Read the Journal
4. Be My Sweetheart

This preserves the logic of the original entrance sequence.

---

# LAYER STRUCTURE

Recommended logical order from back to front:

### Background

1. blue sky
2. atmospheric texture

### Finale Environment

1. sun
2. rear clouds
3. rainbow
4. landscape

### Finale Character

1. horse
2. Lain Doe Love
3. foreground environment

### CTA Layer

1. Be My Sweetheart
2. Read the Journal
3. Contact Me
4. Follow the Journey

### Proclamation Layer

1. parchment body
2. proclamation content
3. upper roller
4. lower roller

The exact order can be adjusted depending on the final art, but the closing proclamation should remain visually above the finale while it is still present.

---

# TRANSITION RHYTHM

The complete transition should read:

**Full proclamation**

↓

**Scroll begins closing**

↓

**Sun rises**

↓

**Be My Sweetheart rises**

↓

**Read the Journal rises**

↓

**Contact Me rises**

↓

**Follow the Journey rises**

↓

**Rainbow rises**

↓

**Lain Doe Love + horse rise**

↓

**Landscape appears**

↓

**Proclamation fully closes**

↓

**Closed scroll exits upward**

↓

**Final scene fully revealed**

↓

**Major movement settles**

↓

**CTA finale becomes interactive**

---

# ENDING STATE

At the end of the Scene 6 transition:

- proclamation is completely gone
- scroll is no longer visible
- sun is in final position
- all four CTA buttons are visible
- all four CTA buttons are interactive
- rainbow is fully visible
- Lain Doe Love and horse are fully visible
- landscape is established
- clouds continue ambient movement
- major scroll-driven entrance animations have stopped

The Heart Direction journey has now reached its final scene.
