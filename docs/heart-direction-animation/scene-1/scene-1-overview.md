# Scene 1: Overview

**Purpose**

Scene 1 introduces the Heart Direction world and establishes the visual language for the entire website.

The visitor begins outside the experience, standing before the entrance to a symbolic world. Rather than scrolling down a webpage, the visitor is invited to move forward through a heart-shaped portal.

The opening scene should immediately communicate that this website is an interactive journey, not a traditional website.

The overall feeling should be warm, magical, cinematic, and inviting.

# **Narrative Goal**

The purpose of this scene is to transform a static illustration into a living environment.

The visitor should experience three distinct moments:

1. Discover the entrance.
2. Accept the invitation.
3. Cross the threshold into the Heart Direction world.

The transition should feel physical, as though the camera is moving through space, rather than the page simply zooming or scrolling.

# **Starting State**

When the website loads, the entire opening composition is already visible.

The visitor should immediately see:

- Background sky
- Clouds
- Decorative stars
- Left and right cherubs
- “Introducing Lain Doe Love” banner
- “The Heart Director” title
- “Revealing the Invisible” banner
- Heart tunnel
- Walkway
- Enter the Tunnel button

No elements animate onto the screen after loading.

Instead, the world is already complete and quietly alive through subtle ambient animation.

# **Ambient Animation**

Before the visitor scrolls, the world should feel alive through continuous environmental animation.

Ambient animation includes:

- Clouds drifting
- Cherubs floating
- Stars twinkling
- Tunnel lights flickering
- Button lights flickering
- Tunnel glow breathing
- Soft atmospheric movement

These animations should support the illusion of a living world without distracting from the composition.

# **Scroll Behavior**

As the visitor scrolls, the illustration transforms into an environment.

The camera begins moving toward the heart-shaped tunnel while the surrounding illustration gradually clears away.

The visitor should feel as though they are physically entering the world rather than enlarging a flat image.

The complete choreography for this movement is documented in the **Scene 1 Transition** page.

# **Ending State**

By the end of Scene 1:

- The exterior world has completely disappeared.
- The visitor is fully inside the heart tunnel.
- The tunnel fills the viewport.
- The camera is facing forward.
- The next destination is visible ahead.
- The animation settles briefly before Scene 2 begins.

This final frame becomes the starting frame of Scene 2.
