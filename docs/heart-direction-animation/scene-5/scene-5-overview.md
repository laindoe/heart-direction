# Scene 5: Overview

## Purpose

Scene 5 marks the exit from the inner tunnel journey and returns the visitor back into the outer world.

The visitor has already been guided downward through the tunnel by the light from the activated heart, and now arrives at the **Back to Reality** sign.

From this point, the animation reverses the visual logic of Scene 1.

Instead of traveling deeper into the heart tunnel, the visitor now travels **out of it**.

As the visitor continues scrolling, they move through the final heart-shaped tunnel opening and emerge back into the brighter outer world.

On the other side of the tunnel, a new scene is revealed featuring:

- a closed scroll
- floating cherubs
- the next chapter of the experience waiting beyond the tunnel exit

---

## Narrative Goal

Scene 5 has three main story beats:

1. **Arrive at Back to Reality**
2. **Travel outward through the heart tunnel**
3. **Reveal the next outer-world scene with the closed scroll and cherubs**

This scene should feel like a return.

The first tunnel sequence represented entering inward.

This tunnel sequence represents emerging back outward.

The viewer should feel the symmetry:

**Scene 1 = entering the heart**

**Scene 5 = leaving the heart**

The transition should feel intentional and spatial, not like jumping to a new section.

---

## Starting State

Scene 5 begins after the heart’s light has revealed the **Back to Reality** sign.

At the beginning of the scene:

- the Back to Reality sign is fully visible
- the heart tunnel structure is visible beneath or behind it
- the interior tunnel environment is still present
- the outer daylight world is not yet fully revealed
- the viewer is positioned inside the tunnel, facing the exit

The Back to Reality sign acts as the final interior marker before the visitor exits.

---

## Opening Animation

As the visitor continues scrolling:

- the Back to Reality sign remains visible briefly
- the camera begins moving outward through the tunnel
- the nested heart tunnel grows around the viewer as they move toward the exit
- the tunnel opening becomes the destination
- brightness increases as the exterior world begins appearing beyond it

The movement should feel like physically traveling back through the heart tunnel rather than scrolling to a new page.

---

## Tunnel Exit

The animation should mirror Scene 1 in reverse.

The visitor is no longer approaching the tunnel from the outside.

Instead, they are inside the tunnel moving toward the opening.

As the tunnel exit nears:

- the outer heart layers pass around the camera
- the heart opening grows larger
- daylight and the outer scene become increasingly visible
- the interior tunnel recedes behind the viewer

The transition should feel smooth and continuous.

There should be no abrupt cut.

---

## Exterior Reveal

On the other side of the tunnel, the next scene becomes visible.

The visitor should emerge into a brighter environment where the new composition is already waiting.

The first visible elements of that next scene are:

- a closed scroll
- floating cherubs
- clouds
- outer-world atmosphere

The closed scroll is the next major focal point.

The cherubs help establish continuity with the opening of the website while also signaling that a new chapter is about to begin.

---

## Ambient Animation

Once the outer scene begins appearing, subtle ambient animation should already be present.

Ambient animation may include:

- cherubs floating
- clouds drifting
- soft atmospheric motion
- gentle light behavior
- subtle life in the outer-world scene

The viewer should feel that the world beyond the tunnel is already alive.

---

## Scroll Behavior

The first portion of the scene holds the Back to Reality sign and tunnel exit.

The middle portion carries the viewer outward through the tunnel.

The final portion reveals the new scene beyond the tunnel and settles into the next chapter.

The complete choreography is documented in the **Scene 5 Transition** page.

---

## Ending State

By the end of Scene 5:

- the Back to Reality sign has been left behind
- the visitor has fully exited the heart tunnel
- the outer world is visible again
- the closed scroll is now the primary focal point
- cherubs are floating in the composition
- the new exterior scene is established and alive
- the visitor is ready to enter the next story beat

This ending frame becomes the starting point for the next scene built around the closed scroll.

## Scene Handoff

Scene 5 ends with the visitor fully outside the tunnel.

The inner tunnel journey is complete.

The closed scroll and floating cherubs are now fully visible in the brighter outer-world scene.

The visitor’s attention shifts away from the tunnel and toward the scroll, which becomes the focus of the next scene.
