# Scene 5: Transition

# Transition Goal

The transition out of Scene 5 carries the viewer from the interior of the heart tunnel back into the bright exterior world.

This should deliberately mirror the entrance sequence from Scene 1, but in reverse.

The visitor begins inside the tunnel facing the exit.

As they continue scrolling:

- the Back to Reality sign is left behind
- the camera moves outward through the nested heart tunnel
- the final heart-shaped opening grows larger
- daylight becomes increasingly visible
- the exterior scene appears beyond the opening
- the tunnel architecture passes around the camera
- the visitor fully emerges into the outer world
- the closed scroll and floating cherubs become the new focal point

The transition should feel like physically exiting the tunnel, not like scrolling to a new section.

The visual rhythm should read:

**Back to Reality**

↓

**Move toward exit**

↓

**Heart layers pass around camera**

↓

**Daylight grows**

↓

**Exterior world appears**

↓

**Tunnel disappears**

↓

**Closed scroll + cherubs settle into view**

---

# STARTING STATE

The transition begins after the Back to Reality scene has fully settled.

Visible:

- Back to Reality sign
- interior heart tunnel
- tunnel lights
- dark atmospheric environment
- final heart-shaped opening ahead

The exterior world is either:

- barely visible through the distant opening
- or still too small to clearly identify

The viewer is still inside the tunnel.

Ambient tunnel animation continues:

- subtle marquee flicker
- faint interior glow
- atmospheric movement

The Back to Reality sign is the primary focal point.

---

# PHASE 1: BACK TO REALITY HOLD RELEASES

The first small portion of continued scrolling begins the exit.

The Back to Reality sign should remain readable for a moment before moving away.

As the visitor scrolls:

- camera begins moving toward the heart-shaped exit
- Back to Reality sign begins moving upward relative to the viewport
- tunnel opening becomes slightly larger
- exterior brightness increases subtly

The movement should begin gently.

The viewer should understand that they are now leaving the interior environment.

---

# PHASE 2: BACK TO REALITY MOVES OUT

As forward movement increases:

- Back to Reality sign continues upward
- sign gradually leaves the center of the frame
- tunnel architecture becomes the dominant visual structure
- the final opening remains centered ahead

The sign should not:

- fade instantly
- shrink dramatically
- disappear through a hard cut

The viewer is simply traveling past it.

---

# PHASE 3: CAMERA MOVES THROUGH THE TUNNEL

The camera continues outward along the center axis.

The nested heart layers begin passing around the viewer.

Each layer should move at a slightly different visual rate.

## Nearest Heart Layer

- grows fastest
- reaches viewport edges first
- passes beyond the camera

## Middle Heart Layers

- grow next
- create additional sense of depth
- pass beyond the viewport slightly later

## Final Opening

- remains centered
- grows more slowly
- becomes increasingly dominant

This layered movement is what creates the illusion of traveling through space.

---

# PHASE 4: DAYLIGHT BECOMES VISIBLE

As the final heart opening grows, the exterior world becomes clearer.

At first, the viewer may only recognize:

- bright blue
- soft cloud shapes
- warm exterior light

As the opening grows further:

- blue sky becomes readable
- cloud forms become clearer
- cherubs become partially visible
- closed scroll begins appearing in the distance

The exterior scene should already exist behind the opening.

It should not suddenly load after the tunnel exits.

---

# PHASE 5: LIGHTING SHIFTS FROM INTERIOR TO EXTERIOR

The lighting should transition continuously.

The tunnel begins dark.

The exit is bright.

As the viewer approaches:

- daylight spills farther into the tunnel
- interior tunnel edges catch more light
- marquee bulbs become less dominant
- dark tunnel tones soften
- the exterior becomes the brightest region

The viewer should feel physically closer to daylight with each scroll.

Avoid a sudden white flash.

---

# PHASE 6: HEART OPENING EXPANDS

The final heart-shaped opening now becomes the dominant frame.

As the camera approaches:

- opening grows larger
- exterior scene fills more of the heart shape
- tunnel edges move toward the edges of the viewport
- outer-world details become increasingly visible

The heart shape should eventually become too large to see as a complete object.

This is the same visual principle used during Scene 1.

The visitor is now crossing through the threshold.

---

# PHASE 7: CROSSING THE THRESHOLD

The camera reaches the heart opening.

The tunnel architecture passes beyond the viewer.

At this point:

- dark interior occupies less of the screen
- daylight occupies most of the viewport
- heart-frame edges pass beyond the screen
- exterior world takes over
- tunnel lights and interior atmosphere disappear behind the viewer

There should be no visible cut.

The transition is created through:

- scaling
- masking
- depth
- parallax
- gradual lighting change

Opacity can support the effect but should not be the primary transition.

---

# PHASE 8: EXTERIOR WORLD TAKES OVER

Once the heart frame passes beyond the viewport, the viewer is fully outside.

Visible:

- blue exterior sky
- clouds
- floating cherubs
- closed scroll

The tunnel should no longer be visible.

The visitor has returned to the outer world.

---

# CHERUB REVEAL

The cherubs should already be moving before the viewer fully exits.

As they become visible through the heart opening:

- both cherubs are already gently floating
- their ambient movement continues naturally
- they do not animate into existence
- they do not wait until the tunnel disappears before moving

This makes the exterior environment feel like it was already alive before the visitor arrived.

---

# CHERUB BEHAVIOR DURING TRANSITION

As the heart opening expands:

- partial cherubs may first appear near the edges
- more of their bodies become visible
- they eventually settle into the full composition

Their ambient floating continues independently of scroll.

Suggested motion:

- gentle vertical float
- slight horizontal drift
- tiny rotation
- different loop timing between left and right

The tunnel transition itself should not dramatically move the cherubs.

The camera is arriving at them.

---

# CLOUD REVEAL

Clouds should behave similarly.

They are already present and drifting in the exterior world.

As the heart opening grows:

- small portions appear first
- more cloud mass becomes visible
- exterior cloud composition eventually fills the sides of the screen

Cloud motion should continue seamlessly before, during, and after the exit.

---

# CLOSED SCROLL REVEAL

The closed scroll is already positioned in the exterior scene.

It should gradually become visible through the expanding heart opening.

The viewer should first perceive:

- scroll shape
- rolled parchment structure
- central placement

The scroll should remain completely closed.

No unfurling occurs during Scene 5.

Its only job here is to become the next visual destination.

---

# CLOSED SCROLL POSITION

By the time the viewer fully exits the tunnel:

- scroll is centered
- cherubs frame it
- clouds surround the composition
- the scroll is clearly the next focal point

The scroll should not need to dramatically move into place after the exit.

It should already be positioned where the next scene will begin.

---

# PHASE 9: CAMERA SETTLES OUTSIDE

Once fully outside, forward camera movement slows.

The exterior composition stabilizes.

Visible:

- closed scroll
- cherubs
- clouds
- blue sky
- exterior atmosphere

The movement should ease gently into stillness.

Avoid a hard stop.

---

# PHASE 10: EXTERIOR HOLD

There should be a short hold after the visitor exits.

During this hold:

- scroll remains closed
- cherubs continue floating
- clouds continue drifting
- exterior scene remains stable
- no proclamation reveal begins yet

This creates a clear separation between:

**leaving the tunnel**

and

**opening the next message**

The visitor should have time to register that they are back outside and that the scroll is now the next object of interest.

---

# CAMERA BEHAVIOR

The camera should remain centered throughout the transition.

The final heart opening is the camera target until the exterior scene takes over.

Avoid:

- sideways panning
- camera rotation
- abrupt zoom changes
- wobble
- cuts

The viewer should feel like they traveled in a straight line through the heart opening.

---

# MASKING BEHAVIOR

The exterior scene should be revealed through the heart-shaped opening.

The final heart opening acts as a mask.

As it grows:

- more of exterior scene becomes visible
- no rectangular edges appear
- tunnel architecture remains around the opening until threshold crossing

Possible implementation:

- SVG mask
- CSS clip-path
- nested overflow
- reused Scene 1 heart-mask system

The same technical system from the entrance should be reused wherever possible.

---

# SCROLL BEHAVIOR

The transition should remain scrubbed to scroll.

Scroll progress controls:

- Back to Reality leaving
- tunnel-layer scaling
- camera movement
- final opening growth
- exterior reveal
- settling into the scroll scene

Ambient animations remain time-based:

- cherub floating
- cloud drifting
- tunnel flicker

Slow scrolling should reveal the transition smoothly.

Fast scrolling should preserve all essential visual states.

---

# REVERSE SCROLL BEHAVIOR

The transition must reverse cleanly.

If the visitor scrolls upward after exiting:

- closed scroll and cherubs recede through the heart opening
- daylight becomes more confined
- tunnel architecture returns around the viewport
- interior environment becomes dominant
- Back to Reality sign returns

The viewer should feel like they are traveling back into the tunnel.

No scene reload should occur.

---

# IMPORTANT SYMMETRY RULE

Scene 5 should visually echo Scene 1 closely enough that the viewer subconsciously recognizes the return journey.

Where possible, reuse:

- same heart-layer spacing
- same mask geometry
- same center alignment
- same tunnel depth logic
- same scaling relationships

The direction and destination are reversed, but the visual grammar should remain consistent.

---

# TRANSITION RHYTHM

The full transition should read:

**Back to Reality**

↓

**Scroll**

↓

**Camera begins moving outward**

↓

**Back to Reality is left behind**

↓

**Heart tunnel layers expand around viewer**

↓

**Daylight appears ahead**

↓

**Exterior scene becomes clearer**

↓

**Cherubs + clouds appear through opening**

↓

**Closed scroll appears**

↓

**Heart frame passes beyond camera**

↓

**Tunnel disappears**

↓

**Exterior world fills viewport**

↓

**Camera settles**

↓

**Closed scroll becomes focal point**

↓

**Brief hold**

↓

**Ready for proclamation scene**

---

# ENDING STATE

At the end of the Scene 5 transition:

- visitor is completely outside the tunnel
- Back to Reality is no longer visible
- tunnel architecture is no longer visible
- blue exterior sky fills the background
- clouds are visible and drifting
- cherubs are fully visible and floating
- closed scroll is centered
- scroll remains completely closed
- no proclamation text is visible
- camera has settled
- exterior scene is stable

This exact composition becomes the starting state of the next scene.

---

# TRANSITION HANDOFF

The next scene begins with the visitor facing the closed scroll.

The tunnel journey is finished.

The cherubs and clouds continue their ambient animation while the scroll remains closed.

Continued scrolling in the next scene will begin physically opening the scroll until the full proclamation is revealed.
