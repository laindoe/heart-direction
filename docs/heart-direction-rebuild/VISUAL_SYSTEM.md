# Heart Direction — Visual System

## Dominant visual language

The world combines romantic ornament, retro-futurist chrome, cosmic darkness,
early-digital nostalgia, and a glowing tropical grid landscape.

The visual system must feel polished and reflective, not grainy or degraded.
When image iterations accumulate artifacts, regenerate cleanly from the locked
reference rather than continuing destructive edits.

## Palette

Dominant colors:

- saturated lavender-purple sky;
- luminous white clouds;
- turquoise water and selected filled landscape forms;
- magenta and pink grid lines;
- red and magenta illuminated accents;
- pearl chrome and silver;
- black void and black display surfaces;
- selective white linework.

Avoid allowing blue and purple to dominate so heavily that the environment
loses the red/magenta identity. Avoid making the full experience uniformly
pink.

## Materials

### Pearl chrome

Used for the spaceship, portal structure, dashboard, marquee, cherubs, temple,
and ornamental architecture. It should appear smooth, reflective, polished,
and slightly romantic rather than industrial.

### Black glass or display surface

Used for dashboard screens, void-backed interface surfaces, and restrained
modal contrast.

### Emissive light

Red, magenta, pink, turquoise, and white glows should be selective. Maintain
dark and neutral areas so illuminated elements retain hierarchy.

### Water

Turquoise reflective water is the continuous physical trail. It should sell
the sensation of a swan-tunnel ride: the ship floats and moves forward instead
of flying freely.

## Opening exterior composition

- Mobile portrait is the primary composition.
- Viewpoint begins near water level.
- The heart tunnel dominates the upper and middle frame.
- The one-seat spaceship is centered in the lower frame.
- The outer world is daytime.
- The sky is saturated lavender-purple rather than literal blue.
- White clouds surround the portal and help it feel grand.
- Grid hills and palm trees create a promenade on both sides.
- Hills and palm forms may use turquoise fill with magenta/pink grid lines.
- The heart-tunnel interior is a dark void.
- Repeating inner ribs glow red or magenta.
- Side bulbs on the outer heart frame glow white.
- A heart jewel sits comfortably below the HEART DIRECTION marquee without
  overlapping it.
- Alien cherubs flank or hover above the marquee.
- GET IN appears in or across the reflective water and receives a restrained
  glow/ripple treatment.

## Spaceship exterior

- One seat only.
- From behind, only the back of the chair is visible.
- Pearl-chrome body with restrained red/magenta illumination.
- Open visual space; avoid excessive structural bars around the canopy.
- Dashboard geometry must visually relate to the approved cockpit reference.
- The vehicle floats on the water.

## Cockpit

- First-person forward view.
- No seat obstructing the interface.
- Large window area above the dashboard.
- Broad pearl-chrome dashboard top.
- Centered heart ornament.
- Large black central display.
- Four clear physical controls.
- Persistent destinations: Saturn, Moon, Store, Socials.
- Do not add decorative side viewport rails that unnecessarily close the view.

## Tunnel and void

- Daylight is left behind as the ship looks forward into the night tunnel.
- Chrome ribs repeat in perspective.
- The water continues below the ship.
- Faint clouds pass through the void.
- Grid hills and grid palm trees remain along the route.
- The first interior arch reads “Welcome to Lain Doe Love's Heart.”
- Alien cherubs hover above that sign.
- Saturn and the Moon remain visible far in the background, positioned so
  they read as stable destinations rather than trail encounters.
- Avoid decorative swirling grid lines across the top of the void.

## Artifact language

Use physical media objects with late-1990s and early-2000s character:

- floating papers;
- notebooks and journals;
- CDs and discs;
- microphones;
- VHS and DVD references;
- analog/digital playback devices;
- period display objects;
- photographs and artwork.

Objects must face the visitor enough to be readable and selectable. Space them
widely, usually 2–3 per architectural arch.

## Destination differences

### Saturn

Simpler surface and environment so professional projects remain the focal
point. The visitor is on Saturn; do not place a separate Saturn planet behind
the work. One continuous ring may stretch over the trail. Project images face
the viewer and float along a forward-scrolling path.

### Moon

Pearl-chrome philosophy temple. Four simple elemental panels are aligned on
the wall directly in front of the visitor. No floating papers and no central
object blocking the panels.

### Store

Clean, restrained void background and a familiar product grid. Reduce scenic
competition so shopping behavior is immediately understandable.

## Motion tone

Motion is restrained and atmospheric:

- soft water ripples;
- reflected GET IN glow;
- subtle spaceship illumination;
- light cherub hovering;
- gentle cloud movement;
- portal and marquee shimmer;
- deliberate forward camera movement;
- a short glitch/pull-through transition.

Avoid constant aggressive movement, elastic easing, or effects that reduce
readability.

## Reference-management rule

Approved images must be copied into a repository or production reference
folder with meaningful filenames before an implementation depends on them.
Conversation attachments and generation IDs are not durable source files.
