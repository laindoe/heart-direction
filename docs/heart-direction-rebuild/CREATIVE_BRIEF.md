# Heart Direction — Creative Brief

## Purpose

Heart Direction is an immersive library of Lain Doe Love's heart, mind,
philosophy, creative practice, professional body of work, products, media, and
expanding worlds.

It is not a conventional portfolio and it is not part of DoeSpace. It should
feel like entering a romantic retro-futurist universe and choosing how deeply
to explore it.

## First-minute outcome

The visitor should feel:

> I want to explore every world.

The launch homepage speaks first to curious followers and readers. Its first
release is a focused prologue rather than the entire universe.

## Launch scope

The first release contains:

- an exterior invitation and boarding sequence;
- a short cockpit orientation;
- a glitch transition through the heart tunnel;
- a guided water trail through the void;
- approximately 5–7 carefully selected artifacts;
- mostly writings, with a few media artifacts;
- a functional starter collection on Saturn;
- a functional starter collection on the Moon;
- a traditional store experience;
- persistent social links;
- one world portal: a small Funemployed threshold teaser;
- an ending that acknowledges the current edge of the trail and invites the
  visitor to follow or subscribe for what comes next.

The initial writings will be chosen and supplied by Lain Doe Love.

## Experience principles

- Guided path with places to pause and explore.
- Discovery without confusion.
- Immersive without sacrificing mobile performance.
- Romantic and emotionally inviting rather than cold science fiction.
- Playful interactivity that supports the content rather than distracting
  from it.
- The spaceship and dashboard provide continuity across destinations.
- The main trail is the library of heart and mind; destinations are optional
  detours rather than interruptions.

## Core content model

### The main trail: heart and mind library

The artifacts are not limited to unfinished projects. They may contain:

- essays;
- journal entries;
- philosophies;
- creative-process notes;
- finished artwork;
- audio;
- music;
- video;
- previews of larger worlds;
- other records from Lain Doe Love's creative life.

The physical object communicates the media type. Papers hold writing; CDs or
related analog objects hold audio; video can live inside early-digital or
late-1990s/early-2000s display objects. Avoid relying only on obvious generic
icons.

### Saturn: body of work

Saturn contains completed professional projects created across disciplines,
including graphic design, creative direction, set design, and related work.
The starter collection should represent the strongest work across disciplines.

### The Moon: philosophy and About archive

The Moon contains four elemental chapters:

- **Earth** — About Lain Doe Love, grounded reality, and a path to the Store.
- **Air** — Transmuting spirit or energy into matter.
- **Fire** — The mythology of the Lain Doe Love archetype.
- **Water** — What Heart Direction is intended to give other people.

### Store

The Store is intentionally more familiar than the exploratory journey. It
uses a product grid, categories, product details, cart, and checkout path.
Square is the anticipated payment destination, but cart and Square-link
logistics must be finalized before commerce implementation.

Approved category structure:

- **Dream** — spiritual growth, expanded consciousness, and planning the vision.
- **Build** — tools for turning the vision into a product, project, or world.
- **Sustain** — systems for maintaining connection to the dream after reaching it.

### Worlds

Larger bodies of intellectual property appear as doorways, portals, or rabbit
holes along the trail. A portal prompt asks whether to enter or remain on the
main path. Only Funemployed opens at launch, and only as a small threshold
teaser until that world is built.

## Tone

Heart Direction should feel:

- romantic;
- surreal;
- intimate;
- reflective;
- playful;
- otherworldly;
- emotionally authored;
- retro-futurist rather than conventionally futuristic.

The experience should feel like Lain Doe Love's universe, not a generic AI
space website.

## Copy locks

Use the spelling **Lain Doe Love**.

Approved orientation line:

> Journey through Lain Doe Love's heart, mind, and body of work.

Approved first control:

> Prepare for Takeoff

Approved exterior invitation:

> GET IN

Do not introduce a message between the cockpit and the void. The transition is
visual: forward movement, glitch/pull-through, then arrival.

## Not in scope yet

- Final sound design or a sound toggle.
- A complete Funemployed world.
- A large artifact archive at launch.
- Every professional project on Saturn.
- Final commerce architecture.
- Unapproved messaging during transitions.
- Free-flight navigation or unrestricted camera control.
